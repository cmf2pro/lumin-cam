# Code of Conduct

LUMIN CAM is intended to be a professional and welcoming project for machinists, CAM programmers, software developers, students, and manufacturing professionals.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Share reproducible technical information when reporting problems.
- Respect different levels of CNC, CAM, and software experience.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and unsafe manufacturing advice presented as guaranteed safe practice are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project productive and safe.
